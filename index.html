Randomized patients received rezvilutamide 240 mg orally once daily with ADT. This trial studied high-volume metastatic disease and used bicalutamide plus ADT as its comparator.

Biology / simplified

Interrupting the
AR signal.

Androgens can activate the androgen receptor (AR), which regulates transcriptional programs involved in prostate cancer growth. AR antagonists interfere with this signaling; abiraterone acts upstream by reducing androgen synthesis.
